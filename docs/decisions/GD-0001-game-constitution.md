# GD-0001 - Adopt the Hunter Order game constitution

> Status: Accepted
> Date: 2026-07-20
> Decision owner: Game Design Lead

## Context

Early Hunter Order concepts existed across conversation history and a Notion database. They consistently described a living procedural world, broad Hunter identity and community-created history, but mixed approved decisions with proposals and contained contradictions.

The project needs one stable product authority that coding agents can use without inventing gameplay.

## Decision

Adopt [`docs/vision/game-constitution.md`](../vision/game-constitution.md) as the highest product-design authority.

The constitution establishes that:

- the world is the protagonist;
- players exist inside an independent world;
- the world constantly generates opportunities;
- skills are the primary progression axis and loot is secondary;
- exploration, knowledge and accepted risk are rewarded over static repetition;
- tension is the default outside rotating controlled zones;
- rarity creates prestige;
- unknown items require identification and create transport risk;
- defeat is expensive, with a possible limited merchant-recovery event;
- systems should follow systemic naturalism;
- player and clan actions create persistent server history;
- one verified person maps to one Hunter account at initial launch.

## Consequences

- New mechanics must be evaluated against the constitution.
- Agents may not change these principles without a new human decision.
- Notion is no longer an authoritative source.
- Conflicting older notes are superseded by repository documents.
- Detailed balance and implementation remain separate specifications.
- Formal clan-conflict mechanics remain proposals until approved.

## Superseded or filtered statements

- Multiple characters for alternate permanent specializations are superseded by the latest one-person-one-Hunter rule.
- Permanent global species extinction is unresolved because it conflicts with accelerated regeneration concepts.
- Formal duel, delay and invasion mechanics remain proposals.
- The old rule that Notion is the project's source of truth is superseded by the repository documentation workflow.
