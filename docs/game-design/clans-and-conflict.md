# Clans and conflict

> Status: Mixed
> Owner: Game Design Lead
> Last reviewed: 2026-07-20

This document distinguishes approved social goals from mechanics that remain proposals.

## Approved principles

### Stories are social

Long-term play should be sustained by the stories Hunters create together and against one another.

Expeditions, alliances, trade, rivalry, betrayal, protection and competition for rare opportunities are core sources of persistent server history.

### Clans are hunting teams

A clan is primarily a team organized around pursuits. Its capability depends on the specializations, skills, knowledge and logistics of its members.

A clan without relevant expertise should be weaker in pursuits that require that expertise. This creates recruitment strategy, dependency and group identity without requiring every clan to become a formal government.

### Conflict follows opportunity

PvP and clan conflict should emerge from valuable circumstances rather than exist as a disconnected queue.

Examples include:

- competing for a rare target;
- controlling access to a region or route;
- intercepting valuable transport;
- protecting a returning expedition;
- disputing a discovery or contract;
- reacting to persistent ecological or economic changes.

### Return is part of the hunt

Obtaining an item or discovery is not always the end of a pursuit. Returning safely to a city, specialist or clan base can be part of the challenge.

## Proposed mechanics, not approved for implementation

The migrated Notion page described this possible conflict sequence:

1. A Hunter obtains an item capable of strengthening a clan.
2. The benefit is consolidated only after returning to the clan base.
3. The carrier becomes marked or traceable.
4. Rival clans may attempt interception.
5. A rival may issue a formal duel for the captured item.
6. The challenged clan may delay the duel within a limited window.
7. The deadline may transition into an invasion state.

This sequence is retained as a design proposal only. Formal duels, deferral windows, invasion states, item claims and forced conflict are not approved rules.

## Open questions

- clan size and hierarchy;
- creation and maintenance costs;
- shared storage and property;
- base placement and destruction;
- friendly fire and alliance rules;
- PvP consent and regional restrictions;
- conflict declarations and cooldowns;
- transport visibility;
- victory, surrender and recovery conditions;
- anti-harassment and anti-zerg safeguards.
