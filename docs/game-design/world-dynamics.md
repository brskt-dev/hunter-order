# World dynamics

> Status: Mixed
> Owner: Game Design Lead
> Last reviewed: 2026-07-20

This document separates approved world behavior from unresolved implementation questions.

## Approved principles

### The world is alive and independent

The world continues to change without waiting for a player. Ecologies, resources, threats, routes and controlled territory can change over time.

Each server should accumulate a distinct history instead of replaying one identical sequence of events.

### The world stays ahead

Hunters can understand, influence and temporarily control parts of the world, but no Hunter or clan should permanently solve it.

The world must continue to create unknowns through procedural generation, ecological response, mutation, migration, regional change and large-scale events.

### Repetition changes a region

Remaining in one region and exploiting one predictable target must affect local behavior.

Possible consequences include:

- prey migration;
- local resource depletion;
- predator movement;
- species replacement;
- new threats attracted by activity;
- reduced strategic value;
- altered routes and trade demand.

The purpose is not a hidden anti-farm penalty. The purpose is a world that reacts to pressure and creates reasons to explore.

### Controlled zones

Controlled zones are safer areas created by sustained Hunter activity. Excessive hunting or defensive pressure has driven away major hostilities.

They may contain weaker creatures that are defensive or neutral until attacked. They are suitable for new Hunters to learn and progress without removing all danger.

Control is rotational and reversible. When activity and protection decline, wilderness can reclaim the region in stages:

1. minor threats return;
2. stronger predators establish territory;
3. the region becomes fully wild again.

### Persistent consequences

Cities, routes, ecosystems, conflicts and discoveries should preserve meaningful history. New systemic content must not erase the fact that players changed the server.

## Filtered Notion concepts

The initial lore draft included both permanent species extinction and accelerated regeneration that prevents prey from being exhausted. These statements conflict.

The authoritative interpretation is currently:

- local depletion, migration and ecological replacement are approved;
- accelerated world evolution may generate new targets and variations;
- permanent global extinction is unresolved and must not be implemented without a specific decision.

## Open questions

The following concepts remain unresolved:

- the origin event or world rule that explains continuous expansion and change;
- whether geography can change permanently or only regional state changes;
- the simulation boundary between active and inactive regions;
- exact regeneration, migration and mutation rates;
- whether a species can become globally extinct;
- how controlled-zone rotation is selected and communicated;
- how much world-state information is public, discoverable or secret;
- how server history is archived and presented to players.
