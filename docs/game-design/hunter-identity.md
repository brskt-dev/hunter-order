# Hunter identity

> Status: Approved
> Owner: Game Design Lead
> Last reviewed: 2026-07-22

## Summary

Every player character is a Hunter. Hunter is the central identity of civilization, not a job reserved for one social group.

A Hunter is defined by what they pursue, what they can do, what they know and what history they leave behind.

## Identity, not profession

The world does not divide people into Hunters and non-Hunters. Different Hunters build different lives through specialization and activity.

The current specialization model is:

- one permanent primary specialization, chosen after an initiation and
  experimentation period rather than at creation;
- one permanent secondary specialization, unlocked later from the same skill list;
- tertiary skill sets available to all Hunters and not inherited by successors.

Tertiary skills may include cooking, agriculture, construction, gathering, production and other economic or everyday activities.

The full character mechanics — attributes, skills, succession and lineage — are defined in [`character-system.md`](character-system.md). The exact skill catalogues and progression curves are not yet defined. Agents must not invent them.

## One person, one Hunter

The initial product rule is one verified person per Hunter account. In Brazil, one CPF maps to one Hunter.

An identity has exactly one **active** Hunter at a time. Over time it may accumulate multiple **historical** Hunters through succession, forming a sequential lineage — never parallel characters and never a respec. This is defined in [`character-system.md`](character-system.md).

International identity verification remains an open decision.

## Pursuit

To hunt means to pursue something with intention, knowledge and dedication.

A Hunter may pursue:

- creatures;
- artifacts;
- phenomena;
- resources;
- regions;
- knowledge;
- information;
- trade advantages;
- social or clan objectives.

This definition keeps combat, exploration, research, production and cozy play inside the same identity.

## Progression and legacy

Veteran Hunters may distinguish themselves through a combination of:

- skill and combat capability;
- accumulated knowledge;
- reputation;
- access to difficult regions;
- recorded discoveries;
- contribution to a clan;
- expedition leadership;
- persistent server legacy.

No single factor should make every other form of progression irrelevant.

## Rarity and respect

Prestige follows rare pursuits and rare accomplishments.

The most respected Hunter is not necessarily the player with the highest numerical power. Recognition should follow targets, discoveries and outcomes that few Hunters were able to reach.

## Cozy play

Agriculture, cooking, construction, trade, gathering and production are valid Hunter paths. They are not a separate non-Hunter mode.

These activities should support personal goals, the economy and clan expeditions without being forced into constant combat.

## The Summit

The Summit is the only institution recognized globally at the current lore level.

It does not politically govern Hunters or assign their personal paths. Its purpose is to represent essential world-level structures and records.

Expected responsibilities include:

- recognizing titles;
- recording major discoveries and achievements;
- naming important hunts and events;
- recognizing Hunters who changed server history;
- revoking the right to hunt as the in-world expression of account bans.

The Summit observes and records history. It does not control the world's evolution.

## Social order

Hunters are individually free. Social order emerges from agreements, dependencies, communities and conflict rather than mandatory service to one central faction.

Political systems, city governance and formal faction structures remain open design areas.
